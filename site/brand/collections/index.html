AETHERIA / Collection architecture

Five ways
to make light live.

Each collection begins with a different natural or perceptual system. The common language is optical depth, authored structure, viewpoint change and a clear separation between visual study and engineering authority.

Design discipline

A collection is not accepted because one render looks good.

It needs a repeatable grammar: a source idea, an optical behaviour, a structural logic and enough range to become more than one object. Released product claims only follow controlled engineering evidence.
