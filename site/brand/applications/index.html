Selection logic

Choose by spatial behaviour,
not by room label.

Height

Does the work need to reveal itself across one plane or several levels?

Movement

Will people circulate around it, beneath it, or mainly view it from a fixed position?

Density

Should the light define a focal object, a field, a threshold or a quieter atmosphere?

Authority

Is the chosen direction still a visual study, or does a controlled engineering record exist?
