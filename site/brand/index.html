01 / Approach

Not a catalogue of lamps.
A language for living light.

AETHERIA begins with natural systems, then abstracts them through optics, motion, topology and structure. The goal is not literal decoration. The goal is an object that changes with space, time and viewpoint.

VORTEX is the first engineered fixture on the platform. The wider collection studies test how the same philosophy can become orbit, current, branching growth, threshold and falling light.

03 / Platform

Beautiful enough to desire.
Rigorous enough to build.

AETHERIA is being developed as a design and engineering platform, not just a rendering exercise. Product truth, presentation studies, geometry, photometry and interchange outputs are kept as distinct domains.
