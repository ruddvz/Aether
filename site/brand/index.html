01 / Approach

Not a catalogue of lamps.
A language for living light.

AETHERIA begins with natural systems, then abstracts them through optics, motion, topology and structure. The goal is not literal decoration. The goal is an object that changes with space, time and viewpoint.

VORTEX is the first implemented fixture programme. The wider collection studies test how the same philosophy can become orbit, current, branching growth, threshold and perceptual motion.

04 / Platform

Beautiful enough to desire.
Rigorous enough to build.

AETHERIA is being developed as a design and engineering platform, not just a rendering exercise. Product truth, presentation studies, geometry, photometry and interchange outputs are kept as distinct domains.

01

Design language

Nature is abstracted through optics and motion rather than copied literally.

02

Engineering authority

Controlled product data stays separate from visual studies and concept imagery.

03

Digital review

Interactive viewers, geometry inspection and versioned product records support design review.

04

Evidence before claims

Measured photometry, mass, certification and physical validation are not implied before evidence exists.
