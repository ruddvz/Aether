# RFQ-KIN-FAB-01 — VX4800 kinetic carrier prototype fabrication capability enquiry

Status: **prepared-not-sent**  
Fixture: AETHERIA VORTEX VX4800-BF-01  
Engineering revision: 1.3.0

Primary route: Bormill Inc.

## Enquiry purpose

We are preparing a prototype rotating-carrier mechanism for a large architectural kinetic-lighting fixture and are requesting a fabrication-capability review. Your published large vertical turning, fabrication/welding and prototype-machining capabilities appear potentially relevant, but this enquiry is specifically intended to determine whether the required distortion control, machining, inspection, traceability and proof work can be supported.

This is not a purchase order and does not contain released manufacturing geometry.

## Controlled coordination information

- fixed canopy envelope: 2400 x 1500 x 150 mm
- rotating carrier coordination envelope: approximately 2260 x 1330 mm
- vertical rotation axis
- carrier speed range: 0.08 to 0.65 rpm; nominal 0.36 rpm
- 240 controlled suspension exit coordinates must remain unchanged
- 14 fixed accent-head positions must remain unchanged
- the existing 24 mm carrier parameter is a coordination parameter, not an available complete mechanism stack height
- physical bearing-axis XY datum, bearing dimensions, brake ring, service lock and secondary-retention sections remain unreleased

## Inputs intentionally not supplied as manufacturing values

- final material grades and sections
- bearing-support ring dimensions
- bearing mounting diameter/bolt circle
- bearing mounting flatness tolerance
- drive-ring geometry
- brake-ring radius/thickness
- service-lock pin/receiver section
- secondary-retention clearances/sections
- final weld procedure
- proof loads
- structural reactions

No fabrication dimension should be inferred from the coordination envelope where a controlled drawing is absent.

## Requested capability response

Please advise whether your facility can support the following, and identify which items are in-house versus subcontracted:

1. Prototype fabrication and machining of a carrier/support assembly within the stated coordination scale.
2. Large-diameter/ring machining after welding or stress-relief where required.
3. A controlled manufacturing sequence that limits weld distortion before precision bearing-support machining.
4. Machining/inspection of a continuous or segmented bearing-support face once supplier tolerances are released.
5. Large circular/annular features, bolt circles and concentric features tied to a controlled physical datum after the bearing axis is released.
6. Dimensional inspection of flatness, runout, concentricity, parallelism and positional features, including the equipment/method used.
7. Material certificate/heat traceability and lot identification.
8. Welding procedure and welder qualification documentation appropriate to the final material/process when released.
9. NDT capability or qualified subcontract routes, including which methods are available.
10. Controlled post-weld heat treatment/stress relief if required by the final design.
11. Surface treatment/corrosion protection routes and masking of precision interfaces.
12. Assembly with controlled fastener torque/preload procedures where specified.
13. Representative proof-load or fixture-based test support after an approved test load and method exist.
14. First-article inspection report and as-built dimensional record capability.
15. Revision/change traceability from released CAD/drawing to finished prototype.
16. STEP/DWG/DXF/CAD data intake and any preferred model/drawing conventions.
17. Maximum practical part size/weight and crane/handling constraints relevant to this package.
18. Prototype lead-time assumptions and information required for a formal quotation.

- representative large-ring or large-weldment machining capability evidence
- dimensional inspection/CMM or metrology capability
- welding qualifications/process documentation capability

## Engineering boundary

Publicly stated machine size or fabrication capability does not make a supplier technically approved. Final fabricator selection requires exact released prototype drawings, tolerance review, process capability evidence, structural/test requirements and a reviewed supplier response. No manufacturing geometry or proof load is released by this enquiry.
