# RFQ-KIN-BELT-01 — VX4800 large-ring synchronous transmission application-engineering enquiry

Status: **prepared-not-sent**  
Fixture: AETHERIA VORTEX VX4800-BF-01  
Engineering revision: 1.3.0

Primary route: Gates Corporation

## Enquiry purpose

We are studying a fixed-side gearmotor driving a large passive rotating carrier through a positive synchronous belt / toothed-ring transmission. We are requesting application-engineering guidance for the transmission architecture and the inputs needed for exact selection. This enquiry deliberately does not invent the final torque, belt width or pretension.

## Controlled product information

- vertical-axis rotating carrier
- carrier speed range: 0.08 to 0.65 rpm; nominal 0.36 rpm
- rotating carrier coordination envelope: approximately 2260 x 1330 mm
- drive transmission is mechanically separate from the primary bearing gravity-support path
- positive transmission is preferred over friction drive
- smooth jerk-limited or equivalently smooth start/stop is under development
- transmission failure must not remove the independent carrier holding/service-restraint architecture
- belt pretension, tangential force and tensioner/idler reactions will be included in bearing/support calculations

## Inputs intentionally not supplied as design values

- final drive torque and peak transient torque
- exact speed ratio
- drive-ring pitch diameter
- belt tooth family/pitch
- belt width
- installation pretension
- sprocket size
- approved acceleration/jerk profile
- final bearing/support stiffness

Please state assumptions explicitly and distinguish preliminary estimates from exact application calculations.

## Requested application-engineering response

Please advise:

1. Which synchronous-belt family/families should be evaluated for a large-diameter very-low-speed ring drive after the torque envelope is released.
2. The complete input set required for tooth-family, pitch, sprocket, ring and belt-width selection.
3. Minimum tooth engagement and anti-ratcheting/tooth-jump requirements for start/stop and abnormal load cases.
4. Recommended installation-tension calculation and acceptable tension range for an exact selected belt.
5. How pretension, tight/slack-side tension and transient separation forces should be converted into bearing/support reactions.
6. Permissible sprocket/ring runout, concentricity, parallelism and alignment for the proposed family.
7. Large ring/toothed-ring fabrication and inspection requirements, including tooth geometry tolerances.
8. Tensioner/idler architecture guidance if required and the reactions that must be carried into the structural model.
9. Minimum pulley/sprocket sizes, wrap angle and any reverse-bend restrictions.
10. Environmental, wear, contamination and maintenance considerations.
11. Noise considerations for slow architectural interior operation.
12. Failure/wear indicators that should be incorporated into inspection or commissioning.
13. Exact CAD/drawing data for any proposed belt/sprocket/ring interface.
14. Which values are published, application-calculated or estimates.

## Required submittals for technical comparison

For any exact proposed transmission configuration, please provide where available:

- exact belt family and part/configuration code
- exact sprocket/ring tooth geometry and mating requirements
- application sizing calculation
- installation-tension calculation/method
- alignment/runout requirements
- dimensional drawing and CAD
- maintenance/inspection documentation
- declared assumptions and missing inputs

## Engineering boundary

No belt family, width, tooth pitch, ring diameter or pretension value is selected by this enquiry. Final transmission selection remains blocked until drive torque, transient dynamics, exact ratio/package, bearing/support reactions and prototype evidence are controlled.
