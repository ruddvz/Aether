# RFQ-KIN-BRG-01 — VX4800 kinetic carrier bearing application-engineering enquiry

Status: **prepared-not-sent**  
Fixture: AETHERIA VORTEX VX4800-BF-01  
Engineering revision: 1.3.0

Primary route: Kaydon Bearings  
Alternate route: Schaeffler Canada Inc.

## Enquiry purpose

We are developing a continuously rotating architectural kinetic-lighting carrier and are requesting application-engineering input for the primary vertical-axis bearing architecture. This is a pre-prototype enquiry. We are not requesting a final bearing selection from assumed loads.

## Controlled product information

- vertical rotation axis
- carrier speed range: 0.08 to 0.65 rpm; nominal 0.36 rpm
- rotating carrier coordination envelope: approximately 2260 x 1330 mm
- 240 suspended assemblies rotate with the carrier
- no planned rotating electrical loads
- fixed-side powered architecture; no slip ring required by the current concept
- the bearing is the primary gravity/radial/overturning support path
- the motor/reducer must not act as primary vertical support
- secondary retention is a separate normally-clear abnormal-failure containment system

## Inputs intentionally not supplied as design values

The following are not yet released and must not be inferred as final values:

- total installed rotating mass
- rotating-assembly center of gravity
- axial/radial/overturning design-load envelope
- dynamic amplification and suspended-field transient loads
- final bearing mounting diameter or bolt circle
- support-ring stiffness/flatness result
- final fastener grade/preload

Please state any preliminary assumptions you need and keep them visibly separate from published or calculated bearing capability.

## Requested application-engineering response

Please advise:

1. Which bearing family or families you would study for very-low-speed continuous vertical-axis rotation with combined axial, radial and overturning load.
2. The complete application inputs you require before an exact variant can be sized.
3. Your combined-load/static/dynamic life methodology and any low-speed/oscillatory or application-specific limitations that matter here.
4. Starting/running torque data and the conditions under which those values are valid.
5. Required mounting-face flatness, parallelism, rigidity and circumferential support strategy for the proposed family.
6. Fastener quantity/grade/preload or tightening-method requirements once an exact variant is proposed.
7. Permissible mounting/runout behavior and recommended post-installation torque/runout verification.
8. Lubrication, sealing, corrosion/environmental and maintenance requirements.
9. Any constraints associated with suspended/tension mounting orientation.
10. Exact mating drawing and 2D/3D CAD for any proposed variant.
11. Replacement/service requirements and any special installation tooling.
12. Which data are manufacturer-published, which are application calculations and which are estimates.

## Required submittals for technical comparison

For any proposed exact variant, please provide where available:

- manufacturer family and exact model/variant code
- current datasheet/catalog revision
- exact mating/installation drawing
- STEP/IGES or equivalent CAD
- installation and maintenance manual
- application calculation or selection report
- explicit assumptions used by your application engineer
- load/torque/life results tied to the exact proposed variant and stated conditions

## Engineering boundary

A recommendation will be treated as a supplier input, not a VX4800 component selection. Final selection remains blocked until controlled mass/CG, design-load cases, support calculations, tolerance closure and prototype evidence are released.
